How the AI decides

55% learning-goal fit + 45% teaching-exchange fit, with a bonus for exact skill overlaps. Scores are calculated from real member profiles.

STUDENT EXPERIENCE INSIGHTS

Feedback Analyzer.

Understand the sentiment in feedback you receive, spot recurring themes, and identify practical ways to improve your skill-sharing sessions.

✦ Sentiment analysis
How the analysis works

SkillSwap analyses the comments you have received using a sentiment lexicon with negation and intensity handling, blends the text score with the review's star rating, and tags recurring feedback themes. Your received reviews are visible only to you here.

NLP · Sentiment scoring · Aspect analysis
